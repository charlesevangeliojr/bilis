01 / DOWNLOAD

Stream. Scroll. Play.

Download speed affects how quickly videos load, pages open, and files arrive. Higher is faster.

Measured in megabits per second ↘
02 / UPLOAD

Send without the wait.

Upload speed matters for video calls, sharing photos, and sending files to the cloud. Higher is faster.

Measured in megabits per second ↗

Stay in the moment.

Ping is the time it takes for a response. A lower number means less delay in games and conversations.
